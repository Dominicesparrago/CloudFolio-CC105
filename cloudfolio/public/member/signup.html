1. Introduction

CloudFolio is a digital Library Management System (LMS) operated to facilitate the cataloguing, borrowing, and return of library materials. These Terms and Conditions ("Terms") govern access to and use of the CloudFolio platform by registered library members ("Borrower", "User", or "You").

These Terms apply exclusively to borrowing activities and shall be in force for every loan transaction created through CloudFolio.

2. Eligibility and Registration

To borrow materials through CloudFolio, you must:

Be a duly registered member of the institution's library.
Provide accurate personal information during account registration.
Keep your account credentials confidential and not share them with others.
Be at least 13 years of age, or have parental/guardian consent if younger.

CloudFolio reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts that violate registration policies or provide false information.

3. Borrowing Rules and Loan Periods

3.1 Loan Duration

Each borrowing transaction is assigned a specific Loan Start Date and Loan Due Date at the time of checkout. Standard loan periods:

General Circulation Materials: 7 calendar days
Reference and Reserve Materials: 3 calendar days
Special Collections: As specified per item

3.2 Renewal Policy

A borrower may renew a loan once, provided:

No hold requests have been placed on the item by another borrower.
The item has not already been renewed previously.
The renewal request is made before the original due date.
The borrower has no outstanding fines or account suspensions.
4. Overdue Materials and Fine Computation

A material is considered overdue when it has not been returned by 11:59 PM on its Loan Due Date. Fines begin accruing automatically starting at 12:00 AM on the first day following the due date.

4.1 Fine Rate Schedule

4.2 Fine Computation Formula

Total Fine = Fine Rate Per Day × Number of Overdue Days (not to exceed the applicable cap)

Example: A General Circulation book due May 8, 2026 and returned May 15, 2026 is 7 days overdue. Fine = 7 × ₱5.00 = ₱35.00 (at cap for that tier).
5. Notifications and Reminders

CloudFolio sends automated notifications to your registered email at these intervals:

3 days before due date — Reminder notice
1 day before due date — Urgent reminder
On the due date — Final return reminder
1 day after due date — Overdue notice with fine accrual start
Every 7 days thereafter — Escalating overdue notice

Failure to receive a notification does not exempt a borrower from applicable fines. It is the borrower's responsibility to track their due dates through the CloudFolio dashboard.

6. Payment of Fines

6.1 Payment Methods

CloudFolio online payment portal (GCash, Maya, credit/debit card)
Over-the-counter payment at the library circulation desk
Institutional billing (for employees or students under institutional accounts)

6.2 Account Hold

Borrowers with unpaid fines of ₱100.00 or more will have their borrowing privileges suspended until all outstanding balances are cleared.

6.3 Receipts and Records

All fine payments are recorded in CloudFolio. Digital payment confirmations are sent to the borrower's registered email automatically.

7. Lost, Damaged, or Unreturned Materials

If a borrowed item is lost, damaged beyond normal wear, or not returned within 30 days past the due date:

The borrower must pay the full replacement cost of the item.
All accrued fines up to the 30-day cap remain payable in addition to the replacement cost.
The borrower's account will be suspended until full payment is made.
Repeated instances may result in permanent account deactivation.

Replacement cost is the current market price or last known purchase price of the item, plus a 20% administrative handling fee.

8. Dispute Resolution

Borrowers who believe a fine was incorrectly assessed may:

Submit a Dispute Request via the CloudFolio Help & Support portal within 14 days of the fine being assessed.
Provide supporting documentation (return receipt, system error logs, or proof of timely return).
Await a response from library administration within 5 business days.

Decisions by the library administration are final. CloudFolio system logs (timestamps, transaction IDs, user activity records) are considered the primary source of truth in all disputes.

9. User Responsibilities

By using CloudFolio, you agree to:

Return borrowed materials on or before the Loan Due Date.
Handle all borrowed materials with care and return them in the same condition as received.
Report lost or damaged items to the library at the earliest opportunity.
Keep your CloudFolio account information accurate and up to date.
Promptly settle all outstanding fines to maintain active borrowing privileges.
Not transfer or lend borrowed materials to any other person.
