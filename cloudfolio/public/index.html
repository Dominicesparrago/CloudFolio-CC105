1. Introduction

CloudFolio is a digital Library Management System (LMS) operated to facilitate the cataloguing, borrowing, and return of library materials. These Terms and Conditions ("Terms") govern access to and use of the CloudFolio platform by registered library members ("Borrower", "User", or "You").

These Terms apply exclusively to borrowing activities and shall be in force for every loan transaction created through CloudFolio.

2. Eligibility and Registration

To borrow materials through CloudFolio, you must:

  • Be a duly registered member of the institution's library.
  • Provide accurate personal information during account registration.
  • Keep your account credentials confidential and not share them with others.
  • Be at least 13 years of age, or have parental/guardian consent if younger.

CloudFolio reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts that violate registration policies or provide false information.

3. Borrowing Rules and Loan Periods

3.1 Loan Duration

  • General Circulation Materials: 7 calendar days
  • Reference and Reserve Materials: 3 calendar days
  • Special Collections: As specified per item

3.2 Renewal Policy

A borrower may renew a loan once, provided no holds exist, the item was not previously renewed, the renewal is before the due date, and there are no outstanding fines or account suspensions.

4. Overdue Materials and Fine Computation

Fines begin accruing at 12:00 AM on the first day following the due date.

5. Notifications and Reminders

  • 3 days before due date — Reminder notice
  • 1 day before due date — Urgent reminder
  • On the due date — Final return reminder
  • 1 day after — Overdue notice with fine accrual start
  • Every 7 days thereafter — Escalating overdue notice

Failure to receive a notification does not exempt a borrower from applicable fines.

6. Payment of Fines

  • CloudFolio online payment portal (GCash, Maya, credit/debit card)
  • Over-the-counter payment at the library circulation desk
  • Institutional billing (for employees or students under institutional accounts)

Borrowers with unpaid fines of ₱100.00 or more will have their borrowing privileges suspended until all outstanding balances are cleared.

7. Lost, Damaged, or Unreturned Materials

  • Borrower must pay the full replacement cost plus all accrued fines.
  • Account will be suspended until full payment is made.
  • Replacement cost = current market price + 20% administrative handling fee.

8. Dispute Resolution

Dispute Requests must be submitted via the CloudFolio Help & Support portal within 14 days of the fine being assessed. Administration decisions are final. System logs (timestamps, transaction IDs, user activity records) are the primary source of truth.

9. User Responsibilities

  • Return borrowed materials on or before the Loan Due Date.
  • Handle all materials with care; return in the same condition received.
  • Report lost or damaged items to the library at the earliest opportunity.
  • Keep account information accurate and settle all outstanding fines promptly.
  • Do not transfer or lend borrowed materials to any other person.

10. Privacy and Data Use

Personal data is protected in accordance with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines. Data will not be shared with third parties except as required by law or with the borrower's explicit consent.
